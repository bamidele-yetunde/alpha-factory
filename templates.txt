# ---- Shapes against production correlation (2026-10-21) ----
# Strong consultant alphas kept failing PROD_CORRELATION (0.80-0.99: too similar to alphas already in
# production). These strip the common part most production alphas share (short-term reversal, size,
# momentum, the price move itself) with regression_neut / vector_neut, or use other mechanics (scaling,
# quantiles, volume-conditioned trading, covariance with returns). Each was simulated once on BRAIN first.

regression_neut(group_zscore(ts_backfill({field}, 63), {group}), group_zscore(ts_mean(returns, 252), {group}))
